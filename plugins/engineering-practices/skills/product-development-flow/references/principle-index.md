# Principle index

All 175 principles of *The Principles of Product Development Flow* (Reinertsen, 2009), by code and
name, with a one-line gist in our own words. Chapter 1 sets out the problem and has no numbered
principles; chapters 2–9 hold the codes below.

The codes and names follow a CC BY-SA 4.0 study transcription
(<https://schuchert.github.io/wikispaces/pages/PrinciplesOfProductDevelopmentFlow>). Its counts add up
to the book's 175, but it is a secondary source: check the book before quoting a code in anything
formal. The gists are paraphrases, not the book's wording.
